`uv.lock`. Anything not confirmed is marked UNVERIFIED. Library signatures were last
re-checked on 2026-10-24 by introspecting the installed packages: typer 0.27.3, rich 15.0.0,
pydantic 2.14.1, pydantic-settings 2.16.1, scikit-learn 1.9.1, numpy 2.5.4, spaCy 3.8.16,
presidio-analyzer 2.2.362, presidio-anonymizer 2.2.364, jsonschema 4.26.0.
